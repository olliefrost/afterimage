# Tutorial Room 1: "First Clone"

**Purpose:** Introduce the core loop (cloning and cooperating with your past self) with no text-heavy tutorial, using level design and visual cues only.

**Mechanics taught:**
- Exploring and following visual hints
- Pressing a button
- Using the clone button to replay your previous run
- Cooperating with your own clone to solve a puzzle

## Room layout

- **Spawn area:** Small, mostly empty room with little to look at, so the player's attention is pushed toward the hint.
- **Door:** Semi-hidden and shadowed so it doesn't read as the obvious route.
- **Hole:** Bottom is hidden in shadow. A hint (dots or a light trail) leads the eye towards it, since nothing else in the room is worth investigating.
- **Pit:** Contains a single button.

## Step-by-step flow

1. **Spawn.** The player enters the room and sees the shadowed door and the hole with its hint. The door isn't an obvious exit.
2. **Investigate.** The player follows the hint and jumps into the hole.
3. **Reveal.** The bottom of the pit shows a lone button. The player is prompted to press it, and the clone button in the corner pulses to suggest it's worth using.
4. **Button press.** A red signal travels along a wire (under the floor through piping, or along the wall) up to the door and opens it.
5. **Clone.** The player hits the clone button and returns to the spawn point.
6. **Watch the past self.** The player sees their previous run play out: the clone jumps into the hole, presses the button, and the signal opens the door.
7. **Exit.** The door is now clearly the right way to go, signposted by light coming through or a small trail. The player leaves through it and enters the next tutorial level.

## Teaching beats

| Moment | What the player learns |
|---|---|
| Hint leading to the hole | Follow visual cues to find the path |
| Button in the pit | Interacting with objects has effects elsewhere |
| Pulsing clone button | The clone mechanic is something to use now |
| Signal running to the door | Cause and effect is shown visually |
| Watching the clone repeat the run | Clones replay your earlier actions |
| Door open and lit | Clear payoff, go this way |

## Art and feedback notes

- Use shadow and lighting to hide the door and hole bottom without making them unfair.
- The red signal should be clearly visible and readable as it travels, since it carries the cause and effect.
- Door open state needs a strong visual change (light spill or a trail).

## Open questions

- How long does the clone replay run, and does it loop or end once the button is pressed?
- Should the pit be escapable by the player, or is the clone button the only way out?
- Is there any failsafe hint if the player doesn't find the hole after a while?
