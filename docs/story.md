# Story and characters

## Backstory

As children, the player and her sister were test subjects in a secret laboratory. The experiments gave the player her time technology: an arm implant that lets her record her actions and replay them as afterimages (the clones). She escaped. Her sister did not.

## Premise

Years later she breaks back into the now deserted, overgrown facility to find and rescue her sister, who she believes is trapped deep inside. She works through the puzzle chambers using her implant.

It is a trap. The sister at the end is a hologram, a cage drops, and scientists have been watching the whole experiment from behind glass (see [the ending](levels/05-ending.md)). It may be that the whole thing was an illusion created by the lab.

## Setting

- **Where:** a lab that blends modern tech with dungeon, now abandoned and overgrown.
- **When:** a post-technology world.
- **Tone:** quiet and lonely, with half-broken relic tech and small guiding lights.
- **Dialogue** is rare: the guard's "HEY! GET BACK HERE!" in the intro, "Oh wait, I can jump!" in Chamber 4, and "Sis?" and "...?" in the ending.

## Intro

The camera starts inside the first chamber and looks at the open entrance door, with only bright light visible outside. A text box shows an unseen guard shouting "HEY! GET BACK HERE!". The character sprints in, the door slams shut behind her, and the camera settles on her for the rest of the game.

It plays on New Game and Restart Game only, and any key skips it. Details are in [Tutorial 1](levels/01-tutorial1.md), step 0.

## The player character

- Long black hair, somewhat scruffy, with the shadow of the hair obscuring her face (blank and anonymous, which also reads cleanly at low pixel resolution).
- A dark greyish tank top and baggy, almost construction-worker trousers in a muted colour.
- A shoulder or upper-arm implant: a central diamond-ish part with geometric lines veining out around it, glowing with the occasional particle effect.
- A rugged, escaped-lab-subject look.

The implant's glow is the neutral white light the player carries; each clone glows in its own slot colour.
